# Best Eleven working contract

Read README.md, docs/METHODOLOGY.md and docs/ASSURANCE.md before changing behavior. The current status is a working research prototype, not an accepted real-data result.

National teams across Test, ODI and T20 take priority over franchise and fantasy expansion. Keep formats, source provenance, historical time cutoffs and role requirements explicit. Do not turn missing tracking, availability, physical styles or appointment records into inferred facts.

Keep one canonical ingestion and scoring implementation shared by the API, CLI, notebook and preview exporter. The preview is a saved synthetic view, not a second model. Never replace failed source retrieval or infeasible selection with convincing-looking fabricated results.

Tests must remain network-free. Use source mocks and isolated fixtures for tests; real-source acquisition is an explicitly requested operation. Preserve raw evidence and revision history with a proportionate retention policy. Do not publish provider data under the code licence.

Where the actual OmniMind runtime is available, route this project through its installed entry point and current rules. Do not invent lanes, peer reviews or acceptance. Independent cross-provider review is still owed. Refer to the assurance document for material open gates rather than reconstructing project status from aspirational prose.
