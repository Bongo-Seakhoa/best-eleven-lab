    WHAT “BEST” MEANS HERE

    A decision you can question.

    01

    Quality is not a career total.

    Runs per dismissal and scoring rate describe batting. Wicket rate and economy describe bowling. Format-specific blends and shrinkage reduce the dominance of tiny samples.

    Five-year-era peer baselines, with batting-order bands, offer a first comparison across time. Sparse cells fall back to a broader peer pool. This is not complete adjustment for eras, opposition or conditions.

    02

    Your preferences stay separate.

    Observed experience uses a saturating match-count score. Leadership uses documented captain and vice-captain appointments, not an invented assessment of character.

    The XI is the optimum of an explicit, role-constrained scoring rule. Change that rule and the team may change. Neither the score nor scenario selection frequency is a probability.

    03

    Keep the unsuccessful attempts.

    Matchups include all recorded deliveries, including bowlers who never dismissed a batter, dots and boundaries. Dismissal-type charts have a different denominator and are labelled accordingly.

    Full exposure corrects a catches-only view, but cannot make the observed field, bowler choice or game state random. Profiles show associations, not causal weaknesses.

    04

    Do not backtest with tomorrow.

    An exclusive as-of date admits only matches whose recorded final day is in the past. Metadata must have been observed before that cutoff. Formats and real versus synthetic evidence stay separate.

    The included rolling diagnostic compares a past-only batter model with a pooled baseline. It does not validate the XI heuristic or tell us whether a team that never played would have won.

    What is deliberately not claimed?

    No complete all-time rankings. No verified verdict on Temba Bavuma. No inferred shot technique, line or length. No causal field optimisation. No automatic fitness, squad eligibility or official fantasy scoring. Those require additional evidence and validation.
