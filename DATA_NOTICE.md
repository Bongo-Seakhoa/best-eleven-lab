The people register page identifies ODC Attribution 1.0 for its register; that statement must not automatically be extended to every other dataset.

Provider archives and local databases are excluded from version control and from the delivered source ZIP. Synthetic data is generated locally with an explicit provenance flag. The offline preview includes only synthetic records, not real player data or private project information.

Metadata URLs record the source supplied by the importer. They are not a guarantee of licensing, accuracy or completeness. Do not ingest credentials, private medical information or unauthorized player-tracking feeds. No provider credentials are required by the initial public-data adapter.
