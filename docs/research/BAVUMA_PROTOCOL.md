# First national study: the Bavuma question

**Status:** preregistered study outline, not executed research and not a verdict.

## Primary question

Within a declared format, role and historical comparison cohort, how does Temba Bavuma’s observed batting contribution compare with that of relevant South African peers? Separate that from whether captaincy appointments provide an additional user-valued selection preference.

The hypothesis may be supported, contradicted or remain inconclusive. Do not choose the period or comparator set after seeing which one produces the desired headline.

## Freeze these definitions before producing a ranking

1. Use Test as the first primary analysis, followed by separate ODI and T20 studies. Do not blend the three.
2. Declare whether the comparison is a middle-order selection decision, a comparison of South African captains as batters, or a complete-career legacy comparison. They have different populations and denominators.
3. Fix the as-of date, available-record window, batting-order bands, minimum exposure and opposition/venue checks. Show country-wide format coverage before inspecting a player’s rank.
4. For the captain comparison, publish whole-career and captaincy-tenure performance separately when verified appointment data and full career data exist. Captaincy is not a causal explanation for a change in batting output.
5. Include dropped, retired and unsuccessful comparators rather than only familiar historical stars. “All-time” is unavailable until the historical source coverage supports it.

## Primary outputs

Publish raw runs, innings, dismissals, not-outs, average, scoring rate and uncertainty beside role/era-normalized measures. Report exposure against individual opponents and opposition teams, with home/away and conditions context where the data support it. Separate peak-window and whole-window analyses rather than selecting whichever favors a player.

Report the performance-only team first, then the experience and documented-appointment sensitivity analyses. Show who changes and why. A leadership preference is not evidence that the player is or is not an effective leader.

## Acceptance before a public claim

Reconcile source totals with independent, licensed or authorized scorecards for the selected periods. Review role and appointment provenance. Audit missing fixtures and unequal coverage. Fit adjustments and tune priors using past-only development data, then evaluate out of time. Check whether the finding survives reasonable comparator, period and normalization choices. Document inconclusive cases and the strongest reasonable alternative interpretation.

The delivered package has not performed these real-data checks, and has no verified Bavuma ranking to report.
